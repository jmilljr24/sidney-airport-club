**BREAKFAST AND AUTO SHOW**

The club will partner with the Algonquin Antique Car Club to bring the
public to the airport on October 4, 2026. The breakfast and car show
remain separate activities, while each adds attendance and visibility to
the other. A shared flyer will present both draws clearly.

The event has overlapping morning activities:

Breakfast setup starts at 7 am, with breakfast from 8 am until noon.

The car show runs from 9 am to 3 pm with judging at noon. The auto show
will be using pavement, grass, tie-down areas and the west hangar area
for vendors and crafts.
